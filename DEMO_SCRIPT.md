# Tiririka — Ultra-Fast 100-Second Demo Script

> **Target Video Length:** 1 minute 35 seconds to 1 minute 45 seconds (gives you a 15–25 second safety buffer under the 2:00 hard cutoff).  
> **Total Word Count:** ~175 words (paced at a calm, natural speaking speed of ~120 words/minute).  
> **Live Site:** [https://tiririka.vercel.app/](https://tiririka.vercel.app/)

---

### Step 1: The Problem & The Hook (0:00 – 0:15) — 32 words
* **Screen:** Browser maximized at [https://tiririka.vercel.app/](https://tiririka.vercel.app/).
* **Say:**
  > *"I'm Prince Nzamuwe. In Nairobi's informal settlements, 20 litres of clean water costs 3 Kenya Shillings—just 0.15 shillings per litre. That is cheaper than a single Solana transaction fee! Tiririka makes pay-per-litre water viable using Solana payment channels."*

---

### Step 2: Open Escrow Channel (0:15 – 0:30) — 31 words
* **Screen:** Click **`Open Payment Channel (0.01 SOL Deposit)`**.
* **Say:**
  > *"The consumer opens a channel by locking a 0.01 SOL deposit into an on-chain escrow vault on Solana Devnet. That is transaction number one—opening up to 1,500 litres of drawing capacity in milliseconds."*

---

### Step 3: Stream Micropayments & Sever Internet (0:30 – 0:55) — 46 words
* **Screen:** Click **`TAP TO FLOW`** (water starts flowing). Then click the green **`Kiosk: Online`** button (turns amber: `Kiosk: Offline`). Click **`5L`** preset.
* **Say:**
  > *"When water flows, the customer's phone cryptographically signs Ed25519 vouchers off-chain with zero gas fees.  
  > Watch this: I cut the kiosk's internet offline. Water keeps flowing uninterrupted! The terminal verifies digital signatures locally in memory in under one millisecond without any network connection."*

---

### Step 4: Threat Defense & Settle (0:55 – 1:25) — 50 words
* **Screen:** In `CRYPTOGRAPHIC THREAT LAB`, click tab **`3. Data Tampering`**, click **`Test Threat Defense`** (`ATTACK PREVENTED` appears). Then click **`Stop & Settle on Solana (1 Final Transaction)`**.
* **Say:**
  > *"If an attacker tampers with payment data, our cryptographic guard blocks it instantly in zero milliseconds.  
  > When finished drawing, we click Settle. The kiosk submits the final voucher in just one closing on-chain transaction—paying the operator, refunding unspent funds, and slashing gas fees by 99.8%!"*

---

### Step 5: Wrap-Up (1:25 – 1:35) — 18 words
* **Screen:** Hover over the settlement breakdown or scroll up to the top banner.
* **Say:**
  > *"Tiririka is fully open source under MIT. We are submitting for Top 3 Overall, DeFi, and Best Beginner Hack. Thank you!"*

---

### Why This Script Will Never Exceed 2 Minutes:
1. Spoken length is **177 words** (the previous draft had 354 words).
2. Leaves **25 full seconds of breathing room** for button clicks and screen transitions.
3. Every single sentence maps directly to a high-contrast visual action on screen.
